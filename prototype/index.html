Exploring the Natural State’s Wild Side

Arkansas truly lives up to its nickname, The Natural State, offering some of the most diverse and rugged outdoor experiences in the Midwest and South. From the ancient, rolling Ozark Mountains to the challenging Ouachita National Forest, the opportunities for adventure are vast.

It is imperative that all visitors practice Leave No Trace principles to preserve these valuable ecosystems.

The Buffalo National River Region: A Paddler's and Hiker's Paradise

The Buffalo National River, established in 1972 as America’s first national river, is a spectacular destination. Its towering limestone bluffs, pristine waters, and accessible gravel bars make it a premier spot for multi-day paddling trips and scenic hiking.

“You haven’t truly seen Arkansas until you’ve floated under the towering cliffs of the Buffalo.”

Many veteran explorers consider the Buffalo a must-do trip, especially during the spring float season.

Key Hiking Trails in the Upper Region

The area around Jasper and Ponca offers incredible access to trails leading to panoramic views. The Lost Valley Trail is a popular beginner-friendly choice, but more challenging routes are available for experienced hikers and endurance athletes.

The primary difficulty in these mountainous areas is often the rapid change in elevation, sometimes called vertical gain by trail runners.

Gear Checklist Tips for Multi-Day Excursions

When planning a multi-day trip, whether on the water or the trail, preparation is everything. Your pack weight is a critical factor.

  • Water Filtration System
  • Emergency Shelter
  • Headlamp with Extra Batteries

Experienced guides often say, Be prepared for everything, expect nothing.

Rock Climbing and Bouldering in the Ozarks

The Ozark Mountains boast impressive sandstone and limestone formations perfect for climbing. Sam’s Throne and Horseshoe Canyon Ranch are internationally recognized climbing destinations.

Types of Climbing and Outdoor Terms
Crag
A steep or rugged rock face or cliff often used for climbing.
Belay
A technique used to control the rope and protect a climber.
Thru-hike
A complete end-to-end journey on a long-distance trail.
